The Vision

Not a ceremony, but an experience.

Catherine and Johnny wanted a day that could hold as much colour and joy as their story. We built the celebration around a single idea: a living palette, where every guest arrived as a bloom in a garden of love.

Even the way guests reached the vows became part of the design, a slow journey across the water into a landscape that felt untouched. So we did not build a page to scroll. We built a walk. [WIS duyet: bo sung yeu cau cu the cua cap doi]

Chapter One - The First Order
origin

It began, of all places, with the same cup of milk tea.

Fig. 01 - The first order

It started with the same order, every visit.

They met through mutual friends, when Johnny became a regular at the milk tea shop where Catherine worked. Over time she recognised not just his usual order, but the quiet warmth in his smile, the way he lingered a little longer each visit. From knowing each other's favourite flavours to discovering the taste of love itself, their story has been one of joy, connection, and sweet surprises.

Chapter Two - A Living Palette
colour

Colour became the language of the day.

The setting

They chose Ninh Binh, where limestone cliffs fall into quiet water.

Towering cliffs, winding rivers, endless green - a backdrop as vivid as they are.

Everyone became a flower, coming together to make a radiant garden of love.

Chapter Three - The Journey
river

The venue could only be reached by boat.

The journey

The journey became part of the day.

Guests boarded small wooden boats that glided through the riverways, cliffs rising on either side, laughter carried across the water - a prelude to something unforgettable.

Chapter Four - The Ceremony
vow

Then, slowly, Catherine appeared - ethereal, like a queen butterfly emerging into her garden.

The walk

Their hands met, hearts full, and they exchanged vows under open skies.

Ninh Binh, 2025 - [WIS: co the thay bang loi cap doi]
