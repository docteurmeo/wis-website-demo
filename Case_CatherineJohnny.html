Some love stories begin with a spark. Theirs began with a cup of milk tea.

The Vision

Not a ceremony, but an experience.

Catherine and Johnny wanted a day that could hold as much colour and joy as their story. We built the celebration around a single idea: a living palette, where every guest arrived as a bloom in a garden of love.

Even the way guests reached the vows became part of the design, a slow journey across the water into a landscape that felt untouched. Nothing decorative for its own sake; every choice carried the feeling of the two of them. [WIS duyet: bo sung yeu cau cu the cua cap doi]

Chapter One - The First Order
Origin
FIG. 01 - The first order

It started with the same order, every visit.

They first met through mutual friends, when Johnny became a regular at the milk tea shop where Catherine worked. Over time, Catherine began to recognise not just his usual order, but the quiet warmth in his smile, the way he lingered a little longer each visit. From knowing each other's favourite flavours to discovering the taste of love itself, their journey has been one of joy, connection, and sweet surprises.

Chapter Two - A Living Palette
Colour

They chose Ninh Binh, where limestone cliffs fall into quiet water.

A region of towering cliffs, winding rivers and endless green, the perfect backdrop for a love story as vivid as theirs.

Colour became the language of the day.

The details

Everyone became a flower, coming together to make a radiant garden of love.

Chapter Three - The Journey
River

The venue could only be reached by boat.

The journey became part of the day.

Guests boarded small wooden boats that glided through Ninh Binh's riverways, surrounded by towering cliffs and the tranquil sounds of nature. Laughter echoed across the water, excitement sparkled in every eye, a prelude to something unforgettable.

Chapter Four - The Ceremony
Vow

Then, slowly, Catherine appeared, ethereal, like a queen butterfly emerging into her garden.

Their hands met, hearts full, and they exchanged vows under open skies.
