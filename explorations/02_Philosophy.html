The first thing we make is time.

Before the room, before the guest list, before the first sketch, there is time. Time to speak plainly, to notice what keeps returning, and to stay with a thought until it begins to open.

01
The first conversation

We begin without a ready answer. A place, a song, a family custom or a colour can be enough to open the room.

02
What returns

The important thing is often the thing that returns: a memory, a gesture, a certain way of gathering people together.

03
The form

Then the work finds its form. Planning, styling and the rhythm of the day begin to hold the same idea.
