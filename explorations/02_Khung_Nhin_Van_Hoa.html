A Glory Story

A house kept in the colors of a painting.

Lily and Antoine returned from France to marry at The Reverie Saigon, and built the day around the paintings of Mai Trung Thứ, a Vietnamese artist who kept his homeland alive in silk and color.

Silk and candlelight
Invitations by hand
His palette on the table
The turning point

The color of his canvases decided the color of the room.

Every invitation painted by hand. Every place card a name, not a number. Đàn tranh at the door as guests arrived. Ivory silk to close, the color he painted his women in most.

Vietnam, in the present tense.

The conversation

A place you have in mind is enough. So is a feeling.
