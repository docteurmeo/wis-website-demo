A Glory Story

One familiar order became a reason to gather by the river — and a day that made room for all their flavour.

The beginning

Hanoi · a milk tea shop

The order
he kept making.

Catherine and Johnny first met through mutual friends. Johnny became a regular at the milk tea shop where Catherine worked; over time, she came to recognise not only his usual order, but the warmth in his smile.

It was an ordinary detail, and wholly theirs. It gave the day its first note: not an invented romance, but two people already known to each other.

A familiar order, held onto
as the story moved elsewhere.

Ninh Bình, in the present

For their destination wedding, they chose Ninh Bình: limestone, water, and a venue set deep in nature. Bright flowers and the guests’ dress code made every arrival part of the scene.

There was one way in. Guests travelled by small wooden boat along the riverways, with laughter carrying across the water.

“With you, love found its place to bloom.”

The ceremony

When the river went quiet.Then Catherine appeared.

As guests settled into their seats, the sound of the river gave way to anticipation. Catherine appeared in the garden, like a queen butterfly emerging into her own place.
