Elopement

A smaller ceremony, with the place allowed to speak.

Best for

Couples who choose intimacy, and a place worth the journey.

Overview

A wedding stripped to what matters - the two of you, a place, a promise. Smaller in size, with the setting allowed to speak, and every hour of the day shaped around the pair of you.

Small in size, never in feeling. Often it takes you somewhere far from home, reached on purpose. [WIS: neu muon hien khoang dau tu cho goi Elopement, cap so.]

What the day includes

Small in scale, complete in care.

01PlanningConcept, venue orientation and search, vendor selection and management, styling and decoration, day-of coordination, and support with transport and accommodation.
02PhotographyCaptured by partner studios - Huk Studio and Mirror Wedding.
03Hair and make-upBy partner artists - Zoey Art, Sally Pham, Thuong Lee and Huy Ji Chun.
04DecorationA custom concept - ceremony, bridal bouquet, reception table and stationery, tailored to the two of you.
