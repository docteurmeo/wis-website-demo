04 — Decoration

Give the room a point of view.

Colour, texture and light composed into a visual world that belongs to the story you are celebrating.

SpaceMaterialLightMemory
A clear role

When the plan is held, let the room speak.

Decoration is for couples — or planners — who have the logistics in hand and need a space with its own emotional temperature. WIS starts by understanding the expectations behind the celebration, then shapes a one-of-a-kind concept around style, story and the people inside it.

Not a borrowed theme. A world with a reason for every choice.

From idea to room

Design needs a way to become real.

Five actual stages make the design legible before it is built. Each one creates something you can see, discuss and carry forward into the next.

  1. 01
    Listen

    Meeting & acquaintance

    We talk about the atmosphere you imagine and the story behind it — finding common ground, your true colour and a direction for the work.

    Output: a shared brief
  2. 02
    Compose

    Concept development

    Mood boards make style, possible concepts, palette, flower language and item details visible, alongside the quotation for the concept.

    Output: a visual world
  3. 03
    Commit

    Agreement

    Once the direction is right, the contract and advance payment let the concept move from possibility into production.

    Output: a locked direction
  4. 04
    See

    2D / 3D visualisation

    The main area is rendered so we can brainstorm, refine and make the most comprehensive version of the scene before anything is built.

    Output: a scene to review
  5. 05
    Make

    Preparation & production

    The design arrives in the room through material, florals, scale and close attention to every last detail.

    Output: the experience itself
Nothing is incidental

What guests feel begins with what they notice.

A palette is not just colour. A flower is not just a flower. The decisions come together in how a room receives people, holds a ceremony and stays in their memory after the last goodbye.
