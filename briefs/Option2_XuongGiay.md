# BRIEF - OPTION 2 "XUONG GIAY" (Paper & Ink / Print Atelier)

*Khoa huong 2026-08-07 sau khi user chot. Nguon: [36 Proposal](../../Research/36_Option2_Direction_Proposal.md). Truc lon: warm-DAYLIGHT material-craft, doi cuc voi warm-dark cinema cua Option 1, van trong dinh vi "We craft glory stories" / Cultural Author / handcrafted luxury.*

## 1. Y niem
Website nhu mot xuong in thu cong ban ngay. Moi dam cuoi la mot **ban in gioi han** duoc luu giu tren giay do. Khach o tam ly tham dinh gu: doc ky, so sen. Do la ly do he nay tinh, sang, co chat giay-muc that, tiet che thay vi kich tinh.

## 2. Design tokens
- **Nen giay:** ivory/do #F4EEE2 (chinh), lop giay sam hon #ECE3D1 cho panel.
- **Muc:** muc nho nau-den #211C17 (chu chinh), muc nhat #4A4034 (than van).
- **Nhan:** son/dat nung #9E3B2E (dau trien, so hieu, gach nhan) - dung RAT it, nhu con dau.
- **Dim:** nau xam #8A7F6B (caption, label).
- Duong ke: 1px #211C17 o 12-16% opacity (net ban in).
- Bong: rat nhe, kieu giay dat tren giay (khong bong cinematic).

## 3. Typography
- **Display + serif than:** Fraunces (variable, optical) - serif tuong phan cao, am, co chat old-style/craft. Dung cho headline, tieu de, than serif.
- **Mono/typewriter:** Courier Prime - cho so hieu ban in, ngay/toa do, caption hien truong, label. Chinh chat "ghi chu xuong in".
- Chu KHONG go size tay: dung mot thang (Display 200 / H1 76 / H2 48 / Lead 30 italic / H3 26 / Body 19 / Small 15 / Label mono 12).
- Khac Option 1 (MADE Mirage lanh sang + sans SweetSans): day la serif am tuong phan + typewriter.

## 4. Signature devices (khac han kho thiet bi Option 1)
1. **Bien Do:** anh dan nhu ban in tren giay - vien seo/deckle rach nhe, xoay 0.5-1.5 do, bong giay mong. (Thay cho full-bleed cinema cua O1.)
2. **So ban in gioi han:** moi story/section danh so kieu "No. 01 / of a limited series" bang Courier Prime + son.
3. **Muc loang:** mot vet muc loang cuc nhe duoi/canh heading (SVG), goi net but long - khong loe.
4. **Con dau trien:** mot dau son nho (vuong bo tron, chu khac) lam moc thuong hieu o goc.
5. **Documentary layer (cho Our Works):** ngay/gio/toa do that bang typewriter canh anh; contact-sheet cuon ngang; grain phim tinh tren anh.

## 5. Motion (tiet che, chat giay)
- Reveal: chu nang len 8-12px + ngam-hien (opacity), kem chut muc loang duoi. Khong wipe, khong scale cinematic.
- Anh: "dat xuong" - opacity + dich nhe 6px + bong giay ro dan. Khong ken-burns.
- Cuon: co the co grain/thay doi anh sang giay rat nhe theo cuon (an toan hieu nang). Khong parallax nhieu lop.
- Preload: mot to giay truot vao + wordmark dong dau, roi lat sang trang. Nhanh, gon, khong bung deck nhu O1.

## 6. Texture
- Nen giay + tho dung SVG feTurbulence (fractalNoise) phu opacity thap + mot lop van soi doc rat mo. Tu tao, self-contained, khong can anh nang.
- Anh cuoi: dung lai bo anh that dang co, phu treatment am (warm/faded nhe) cho hop tong giay, khong de anh ruc lam vo he sang tram.
