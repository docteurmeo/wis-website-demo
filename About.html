About - Since 2009

We do not decorate weddings. We author them.

Wed in Style began with a simple belief - that a wedding is not a template to be filled, but a story only one couple could tell.

For more than fifteen years we have planned and designed weddings across Vietnam, from private gardens in Hanoi to islands reached only by boat. We call ourselves Cultural Authors, because our work is to read a couple's life closely, then write the day around what we find.

What comes from the heart, goes to the heart.

It is the line we return to when a decision is hard. Not the grandest option, not the most photographed - the truest one. Vietnam appears in our work through real place, light and material, never through borrowed symbols.

2009

The year the first story was told.
