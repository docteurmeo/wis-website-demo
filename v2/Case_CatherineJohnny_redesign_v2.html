A Glory Story

Some love stories begin with a spark. Theirs began with a cup of milk tea.

Catherine and Johnny came to us with one true starting point: a shared order at a milk tea shop in Hanoi, long before either of them imagined a wedding day. From there, the celebration found its shape — a destination wedding in Ninh Bình, reached only by boat, with bright florals and a dress code that let every guest feel part of the garden.

I — The First Order

It started with the same order, every visit.

They first met through mutual friends, when Johnny became a regular at the milk tea shop where Catherine worked. Over time, Catherine began to recognise not just his usual order, but the quiet warmth in his smile, the way he lingered a little longer each visit. From knowing each other’s favourite flavours to discovering the taste of love itself, their journey has been one of joy, connection, and sweet surprises.

II — An Enchanted Arrival

With you, love found its place to bloom.

To celebrate their union, Catherine and Johnny chose to host a destination wedding in Ninh Bình — a region in northern Vietnam known for towering limestone cliffs and quiet water, with bright floral decorations and a guest dress code that turned every arrival into part of the scene.

The venue was nestled deep in lush, untouched nature, reachable only by boat. Guests boarded small wooden boats that glided through Ninh Bình’s riverways — laughter echoing across the water, a prelude to something unforgettable.

even the river went quiet
III — No matter where we go, love leads the way

As guests settled into their seats, the sound of the river faded into quiet anticipation.

Then, slowly, Catherine appeared — like a queen butterfly emerging into her garden.

IV — Personal Touches

Two details only the two of them would think to add.

By the end of the celebration, there was no doubt in anyone’s heart — this was, wonderfully, entirely them.
