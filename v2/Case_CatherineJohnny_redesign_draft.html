A Glory Story

A story that began with one small, familiar taste.

[ minh hoạ tay vẽ riêng cho case này — ly trà sữa — chưa dựng, cần theo ILLUSTRATION_DNA.md ]

Catherine and Johnny came to us with one true starting point: a shared order at a milk tea shop in Hanoi, long before either of them imagined a wedding day. From there, the celebration found its shape — a destination wedding in Ninh Bình, reached only by boat, with bright florals and a dress code that let every guest feel part of the garden.

Chapter One — The First Order

It started with the same order, every visit.

They first met through mutual friends, when Johnny became a regular at the milk tea shop where Catherine worked. Over time, Catherine began to recognise not just his usual order, but the quiet warmth in his smile, the way he lingered a little longer each visit. From knowing each other’s favourite flavours to discovering the taste of love itself, their journey has been one of joy, connection, and sweet surprises.

With you, love found its place to bloom.

Chapter Two — An Enchanted Arrival

A place reached only by water.

To celebrate their union, Catherine and Johnny chose to host a destination wedding in Ninh Bình — a region in northern Vietnam known for towering limestone cliffs and quiet water, with bright floral decorations and a guest dress code that turned every arrival into part of the scene.

The venue was nestled deep in lush, untouched nature, reachable only by boat. Guests boarded small wooden boats that glided through Ninh Bình’s riverways, surrounded by towering cliffs and the quiet sounds of the water — laughter echoing across the surface, a prelude to something unforgettable.

No matter where we go, love leads the way.

Chapter Three — The Ceremony

As guests settled into their seats, the sound of the river faded into quiet anticipation. Then, slowly, Catherine appeared — like a queen butterfly emerging into her garden.

Chapter Four — Personal Touches

Two details only the two of them would think to add.

This wasn’t just a wedding filled with pretty decorations — it was a deeply personal celebration, full of the couple’s own signatures. One of their shared loves is tattoo art: for them, tattoos are more than ink on skin, they’re expressions of memory and meaning, so a temporary tattoo station let every guest carry home a small mark of the day.

Another trait they share is a love for spicy food. Wherever they travel, Catherine and Johnny make it a point to try the local heat — so chilli peppers found their way into the wedding decor, a small, spirited signature only they would think to add.

even the river went quiet

By the end of the celebration, there was no doubt in anyone’s heart — this was, wonderfully, entirely them.
