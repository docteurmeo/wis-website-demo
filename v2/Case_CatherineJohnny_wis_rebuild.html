Catherine & Johnny

The beginning

A familiar order. Then a day in the middle of the water.

Catherine and Johnny first met through mutual friends. Johnny became a regular at the milk tea shop where Catherine worked, and Catherine began to recognise not only his usual order, but the warmth in his smile.

Later, they chose Ninh Bình for a celebration held deep in nature. Bright flowers and a dress code made every arrival part of the picture their guests would share.

The way in

The arrival became part of the gathering.

Aravinda Resort sat beyond Ninh Bình’s riverways. Getting there meant boarding small wooden boats and moving together through the landscape before the celebration began.

Water, limestone and the sound of guests crossing into the place gave the day its own rhythm.

The details that belonged

Ink, heat, and a garden made for their guests.

Both Catherine and Johnny love tattoo art, so a temporary tattoo station gave guests a small mark to take from the day. They also share a love of spicy food; chilli peppers appeared in the decoration.

These were recognisable parts of the two people at its centre.

By the end of the celebration, it was entirely theirs.
